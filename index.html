Known hazards

          

            
            
Eddie
Forms. So many forms. Getting caught costs sanity. Also approves your reports, eventually.

          

          

            
            
Clifton
Timesheet audits. Getting caught costs sanity and 20 seconds. His stamp is mandatory.

          

          

            
            
Kevin
A black hole of conversation. Get too close and you're pulled in. You still need his sign-off.

          

          
Managers only see what's in front of them: sneak behind, break line of sight, or stun them with a paper-airplane memo. Running is loud. Stuck in a conversation? Press E to excuse yourself. And Ryan R in IT will help you, just don't bother him too often.
